Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

WeeklyPosts is a private tool used only by its owner, Kevin Hollingworth, to publish his own content.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: the owner's own channel, to update video descriptions, set videos public and add captions.
  • Google Drive: the owner's own WeeklyPosts folders, to read and save media files.

How data is used

Data from Google APIs is used only to publish the owner's own posts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data is collected from anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security, Third-party apps and services.

Contact

Through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/khollingworth.
